# Audio assets and licensing

The **Tablero real** sound preset uses recordings distributed under **CC0 1.0**.

- `public/audio/real-move.mp3`: `sharechess/sharechess/public/sfx/move.mp3`, attributed by the source project's credits to **mh2o**, Freesound, CC0 1.0.
- `public/audio/real-capture.mp3`: `sharechess/sharechess/public/sfx/snap.mp3`, attributed by the source project's credits to **alegemaate**, Freesound, CC0 1.0.

Source documentation: https://github.com/sharechess/sharechess/blob/main/public/CREDITS.md
CC0 terms: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Other audio presets in IB Chess Analyzer are generated programmatically and are not official Lichess or Chess.com audio assets. The default Lichess Standard audio has been identified as non-free in Lichess's own licensing discussion and has **not** been copied.
